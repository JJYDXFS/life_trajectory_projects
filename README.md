# Life Trajectory Projects

Project website for **[Charting Life Trajectory at Scale: A Trilogy](https://jjydxfs.github.io/life_trajectory_projects/)**, a series of studies on extracting and analyzing human mobility and interaction trajectories from news articles and Wikipedia biographies.

## Website

The website presents three related projects:

| Part | Project | Publication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | [CeleTrip: Where Did the President Visit Last Week? Detecting Celebrity Trips from News Articles](./celetrip.html) | ICWSM 2024 |
| II | [COSMOS: Paths of A Million People: Extracting Life Trajectories from Wikipedia](./cosmos.html) | ICWSM 2025 |
| III | [FALCON: When Life Paths Cross: Extracting Human Interactions in Time and Space from Wikipedia](./falcon.html) | Preprint |
